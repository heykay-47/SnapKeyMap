# SnapKey Map

SnapKey Map lets people choose what the Snap Key on Oppo phones does, beyond the options ColorOS offers.

## Language

**Snap Key**:
The programmable hardware key on the Oppo Find X9.
_Avoid_: action button, side key, Plus Key

**Gesture**:
A way of pressing the Snap Key that SnapKey Map tells apart: press, double press, press & hold, or tap then hold.
_Avoid_: trigger, input

**Action**:
What SnapKey Map does when a gesture happens.
_Avoid_: command, shortcut

**System action**:
What ColorOS itself does with the Snap Key, chosen in the phone's Settings rather than in SnapKey Map.
_Avoid_: default action, native action

**Mind Space**:
Oppo's app that stores items a person captures (screens, audio, notes) for later recall.
_Avoid_: MySpace, Mind space

**Mind Space entry**:
An item in Mind Space that looks the same as one the system action creates.
_Avoid_: card, capture, note

**Screenshot preview**:
The floating thumbnail ColorOS shows after a screenshot, with buttons such as Snap to Mind Space, Scroll and Share.
_Avoid_: thumbnail, screenshot toolbar

**Hold to record**:
Recording audio for a Mind Space entry from the moment a hold is recognised until the Snap Key is released.
_Avoid_: long-press recording, voice memo
