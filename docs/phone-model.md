# Find X9 illustration references

The interactive phone is authored in HTML and CSS. No manufacturer photography, logos, third-party 3D models, or paid assets are shipped with it. Both views depict the standard Find X9 in Titanium Grey, not the Pro. Screen content is illustrative, not a reproduction of the device software.

## Official references

- [Product page](https://www.oppo.com/en/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x9/): flat display, symmetrical 1.15 mm bezels, matte aluminum frame and glass back.
- [Specifications](https://www.oppo.com/en/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x9/specs/): body height 156.98 mm, width 73.93 mm, thickness 7.99 mm. The CSS model uses these ratios; camera protrusion is additional.
- [Specifications product image](https://www.oppo.com/content/dam/oppo/common/mkt/v2-2/find-x9-series-en/product/find-x9/black-red-titanium.png): body corners, centered hole-punch camera, upper-left rounded-square camera island, two large circular lenses, vertical telephoto/sensor capsule, and lower-right flash.
- [Snap Key side detail](https://www.oppo.com/content/dam/oppo/product-asset-library/find/find-x9-series/find-x9/en/assets/images-design-zj-card-1-f3f71b.png): frame-colored capsule button. Its position and size are estimated from the official side image: approximately 34.3% down the left frame, spanning 10.3% of the body height. These are visual estimates, not manufacturer CAD measurements.

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Reference images are not bundled. Branding on the original hardware is intentionally omitted, including camera branding. The close-up exaggerates the button's travel to make each gesture legible. The second phone is a separate rear view; it does not attempt to show both faces of one device through each other.
